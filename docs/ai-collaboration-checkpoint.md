# Lab 4 — AI Collaboration Checkpoint

Team A — Aster  
Project: Neighborhood Listing Platform

## Purpose and scope
This checkpoint records AI-assisted discovery work for the two stakeholder groups: property professionals and local sponsors. The interview responses analyzed below were **fictional, generated for a classroom simulation**, not statements from real stakeholders. Their use as assignment evidence is subject to instructor approval.

## ChatGPT question drafting and Gemini critique
ChatGPT helped draft eight interview questions. Gemini reviewed them for leading wording, scope, and privacy concerns.

- **Question 2:** “What challenges do you experience?” presumed problems. Revised to ask how well the process works, and what takes time *if anything*.
- **Question 5:** “How do you check information?” presumed a review step. Revised to ask what steps, *if any*, happen before publishing.
- **Question 7:** Asking for accessibility features prompted hypothetical solution design. Revised to ask about barriers respondents or clients have encountered.
- **Question 8:** Asking how to make “this platform” easier presumed experience with the unfinished product. Revised to ask about the tools participants already use.
- **Privacy guidance:** Avoid client names, private lead lists, proprietary financial details, and account credentials. Participation should be voluntary, with minimal de-identified notes.

The revised set of eight questions is recorded in Section 2 of `LAB4_DISCOVERY.md.`.

## AI Studio analysis of simulated interview notes
Google AI Studio summarized the fictional answers into four themes. Each one was checked against the corresponding simulated response:

| Theme | Supporting simulated answers | Candidate requirement (inference, not validated) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Updating information across multiple places takes time | Q1: websites and spreadsheets; Q2: repeated updates take time | Provide a simple place to edit and manage listing information |
| Core listing information and photos matter | Q4: price, location, and pictures; Q5: checks details and photos before publishing | Show key property fields prominently and include a pre-publication review |
| Reaching local home seekers and receiving inquiries | Q3: local home seekers; Q6: inquiries and contacts | Include location search and consider ways to track listing inquiries |
| Simple navigation and phone usability | Q7: small text and mobile difficulties; Q8: navigation, buttons, easy editing | Use readable mobile layouts, clear navigation, and accessible controls |

## Verification
The four AI Studio themes can each be traced to the fictional interview notes, as indicated above. No real stakeholder evidence was collected during this simulation. The candidate requirements are design inferences, not verified stakeholder requests. Any claim that a theme represents a real user need must be confirmed with an approved interview.
